Translational Medical Intelligence Lab

Welcome to Translational Medical Intelligence (TMI) Lab at Yonsei University. We focus on multiple aspects of biomedical imaging—ranging from data acquisition to image reconstruction, enhancement, and quality assessment.
Our research spans areas such as medical physics, computational imaging, and machine learning to advance imaging technologies for healthcare and broader scientific applications.
